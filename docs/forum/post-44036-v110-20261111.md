**Update, 11 November: fourth patch, the lost prints replayed, release v1.1.0.**

**The two prints abehrman lost now run through the firmware as they were printed.** He shared both G-code files in [u1-klipper#22](https://github.com/Snapmaker/u1-klipper/issues/22), unmodified, from a stock U1 on 2.0.0.205. Replayed on the firmware code of 2.0.0 and 2.1.0, with the Skips where his klippy.log puts them: stock reproduces both faults, the `Move out of range` on Y on one and the stray move across the part on the other; with the #22 patch both files print to the end. The replay is `tests/issue22/replay_real.py`, and its output for each run is in `docs/evidence/issue-22/`.

**A second defect showed up on his 1 November file, and it has its own patch.** With the drag fixed, [U1 Dry Run](https://forum.snapmaker.com/t/43986) still flagged 8 travels after a Skip that cross finished parts almost at print height: the first travel after a skipped object starts from where the head really is, so the slicer's 0.4 mm ramped lift gets stretched over the whole distance. `patches/0022b-exclude-object-lift-before-travel.patch` goes on top of the #22 patch and lifts where the head is before it travels. On that file: 8 such travels on stock and with the #22 patch alone, 0 with both. Its test fails on stock and with the #22 patch alone, passes with it, and two deliberately wrong versions fail, on 2.0.0 and 2.1.0. Offered upstream as draft PR [#28](https://github.com/Snapmaker/u1-klipper/pull/28). This one is offline only: nobody has printed with it yet.

**Release v1.1.0:** https://github.com/DesignMaster-Solutions/u1-fixpack/releases/tag/v1.1.0 (four `.patch` files; checked from the zip, all four apply with `git apply` on 2.0.0 `93cb12f` and 2.1.0 `7ac453b`, 0022 before 0022b). The project site now has a one-page summary with the video and the apply commands: https://designmaster-solutions.github.io/u1-fixpack/ (the link in my previous post went to an empty page until today, sorry about that).

If you skip objects on multi-tool prints and can try a build with the two exclude_object patches on a U1, I would like to hear how it goes, either way.
